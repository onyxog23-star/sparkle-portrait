Sparkle Portrait

Turn a photo into pastel sparkle line art that draws itself, then save it as a video for Reels.

Demo
Format

Playing the demo. Choose a photo to make your own.

On iPhone, tap Save video, then Save Video again in the share sheet to put it in Photos.

How it works
  1. Your photo is scaled to fit and turned to grayscale with a light blur.
  2. Canny edge detection keeps only the outlines. Line detail sets its two thresholds.
  3. Each outline pixel gets a sparkle that flies in on a curve and settles, top to bottom.
  4. The quote fades in and the finished piece twinkles. The whole video is 17.5 seconds.
Everything runs in your browser. Your photo never leaves your phone. Code on GitHub.
